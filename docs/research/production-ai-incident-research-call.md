# Production AI incident workflow research

Morphic is recruiting 15 engineering, AI platform, reliability, and product
leaders for a 45-minute problem interview. The study asks how a real production
AI incident moved from report or trace to code change, regression evidence,
approval, merge, and deployment.

This is problem research before a product pitch. It is not a request for raw
traces and it is not a free-product waitlist.

## Who fits

Every participant must be able to confirm:

- a customer-facing AI agent or AI feature is in production;
- application code and release checks live in GitHub;
- the team had a behavioral production incident in the last 90 days;
- an observability, tracing, or evaluation system is already in use; and
- the participant can describe or change the release workflow.

The initial target is a B2B software company with roughly 20 to 200 engineers.
Larger teams are useful as a separate falsification cohort, but they do not
replace the initial segment.

## What the interview covers

The first 35 minutes reconstruct the participant's last incident and current
workflow without mentioning Morphic. Only then does the researcher show the
evidence-bound release-control hypothesis and ask what is redundant, what is
missing, and whether the problem is budget-worthy.

Use the full
[customer-validation protocol](incident-regression-customer-validation-kit.md)
for screening, questions, artifact handling, and evidence records.

## Volunteer safely

To volunteer in a public recruiting thread before the structured form is
available, comment with only:

- your role category and engineering-team-size range;
- confirmation that a customer-facing AI system is in production;
- confirmation that code and release checks live in GitHub;
- confirmation that a behavioral incident occurred within 90 days;
- the name of the observability or evaluation system in use; and
- whether you will join a 45-minute interview before seeing a product pitch.

Do not post an incident summary or personal contact information. Coordinate
next steps through public GitHub replies until both parties choose a safe,
private channel.

Use the
[public research form](https://github.com/taranggoyal70/Morphic/issues/new?template=production-ai-incident-research.yml)
after it is available on the repository's default branch. The form requests
only public-safe screening information. Do not post customer names, prompts,
messages, traces, credentials, private links, contact details, or proprietary
incident content.

A form, comment, introduction, or expression of interest is a recruiting lead.
It earns no market-validation point. A problem interview counts only after a
qualified participant completes the non-leading protocol and a dated,
repository-safe evidence record exists.
